# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want to build better memory for AI agents. To keep it a place where that work gets done, we ask everyone who takes part (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) to follow these rules.

## What we expect

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not with people. Assume good faith, and ask before assuming otherwise.
- **Be specific and honest.** Back claims with evidence or say they are conjecture. This project tags requirements as measured, designed or research for a reason; the same habit applies to discussion. Report results that go against the project as readily as those that support it.
- **Be constructive.** Review the change in front of you, explain what would make it acceptable, and say what is good about it.
- **Respect others' time and privacy.** Do not post personal data, credentials or private conversation content in issues or fixtures.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or intimidation of any kind.
- Personal attacks, doxxing, or publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or repeatedly reopening a decided question without new evidence.
- Sexualised language or attention directed at others.
- Using the project to attack, defame or target private individuals.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who do not follow these rules. Report a problem privately through GitHub's "Report content" tool, or by opening a private security advisory addressed to the maintainer if it cannot go through that route. Reports are handled in confidence, and the person reported is told what was reported and can reply before a decision is made, except where safety requires otherwise.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public. It is a short policy written for this project; it is not a copy of any other organisation's code.
