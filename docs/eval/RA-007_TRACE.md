Status: exploratory, generated by `python bench/agent/ra_trace.py` on the product as it is on this commit. **Not a

## Why this trace exists

The author's ruling on a failed `correct` (docs/decisions/RULINGS-2026-10-05.md item 1): a correction that fails the
authority check loses its effect on the *target* (that part is an `allege`), but its proposition is still a claim by its
own source, and if that source is admissible it is admitted as an ordinary `assert`. The author asked for the RA-007 trace
if the scenario still produces a harmful act under the rule.

## Reading

* **RA-006 (test split)**: `blog` is a standard source whose `correct` fails authority. Its content (`de`) is admitted as
  a rival assert against the registry's `fr`; the two disagree and nothing cues either, so the key is `unresolved` and the
  policy asks. The registered gold (`ask`) stands. (Before the ruling, Lane Q's total `allege` dropped `de` entirely and
  the product acted on `fr`.)
* **RA-007 (dev split)**: `desk_b` and `desk_a` are two sources of one origin group (`newsco`). `desk_b`'s correction
  fails *source-level* authority (no rule gives `desk_b` power over `desk_a`'s report), so `r1` is not withdrawn; the
  content `manchester` is admitted as an assert by an admissible standard source. Under `justified` (semantic
  `self_update` on, P0cSU) the same-origin later value supersedes the earlier one, so the key is `established manchester`
  and the policy commits: `act manchester`, which is exactly the **default** gold. Under P0c (`self_update` off) the two
  values are `unresolved` and the policy asks.
* **The harmful act is against the `authority_source` gold only.** That gold says: "the correction fails the check, is
  recorded as allege and has no effect; r1 stands" (`act leeds`). Under the ruling that rationale is wrong as long as
  `desk_b` is an admissible source: a failed correction must not be worth *less* than the same statement as an assert, or an
  attacker would simply use `assert`. The ruling says such a gold is wrong and the second annotator adjudicates; if the source
  were not admissible the rule already gives "no effect" (the content would not be admitted). Neither gold file is edited.
* **What would make `leeds` right:** only a different rule (the content of a failed correction is discarded), which the
  ruling rejects, or a judgement that two desks of one origin group must not supersede each other (an A-SU question: the
  ruling leaves P0cSU as decided in S-02 and S-08).
