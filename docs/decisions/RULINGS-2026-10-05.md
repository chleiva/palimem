## Authority and admission

1. **Failed `correct` (RA-006 vs RA-007).** A `correct` that fails the authority check loses its effect on the *target* (that part becomes `allege`), but its proposition is still a claim by its own source: if that source is admissible, it is admitted as an ordinary `assert`. Otherwise a correction from a source would count for less than the same statement as an assert, and an attacker would just use `assert`. Design v0.3's "no effect on admissibility" is amended to **"no effect on the target; content admitted as an assert from its own source"**. `AdmissionConfig.failed_correction_is_allege` means exactly the target part. Under this rule RA-006 answers `ask` (gold stands); RA-007's gold is re-read (if it expects "no effect" from an admissible source, the gold is wrong and the second annotator adjudicates; if the source is not admissible the rule already gives "no effect"). The author wants the RA-007 trace if it still produces a harmful act. Amends S-02. Lane S2.
2. **Source-scope withdraw: no.** A source going bad is an *admission* operation, `exclude_source(from=lsn)`, recorded with reason and admission version, not a report cue. The compat side table covers the 85-query late-assert case. Lane S2.
3. **Authorised dispute (tb-18).** The target's candidate becomes `unresolved` against "disputed"; no value is asserted until confirmation from another origin group or a withdrawal. Compat follows the oracle. Lane S2.
4. **Merge as a Power: yes**, additive, plus a `MergeRecord` type. Lane S1.
